Questions for the Detective

Licensing FAQ

How much does it cost to become a licensee?

$15.99 a month, plus a royalty on each event: $4.50 per ticket when you sell tickets, or 15% of the host fee you negotiate with a venue. Everything else is yours.

How is the royalty paid?

It’s deducted automatically, so there are no invoices to chase or reports to file.

Do I keep tips and merch sales?

Yes. You keep 100% of your tips. You can buy merch from the Sleuth Society store and resell it at your events, and the profit is all yours.

How do my guests buy tickets?

Sleuth Society provides the pay link, so you can start selling tickets without setting up your own checkout.

Do I set my own prices?

Yes. Choose your own ticket price, or negotiate your own host fee with the venue. Sleuth Society only takes $4.50 per ticket or 15% of the host fee, so if you negotiate $3,000, you keep $2,550 (85%).

How many events can I host?

As many as you’d like. It’s customizable and you’re your own boss, and the tools can be reused as many times as you want.

Can I reuse the mysteries and tools?

Yes. Everything in the portal is yours to use as many times as you want, so you can run the same mystery for new guests and new venues.

How much can one event bring in?

One event can bring in up to $5,000, depending on your ticket price, attendance and costs. See The Numbers for illustrative examples. Results vary and aren’t guaranteed.

Who is licensing a good fit for?

Anyone in the events or entertainment world who wants to broaden what they offer: event planners, corporate event providers, trivia hosts, DJs and people who work with venues.
