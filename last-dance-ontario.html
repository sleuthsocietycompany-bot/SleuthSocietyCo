The Case

The Night Someone Doesn't Make It Off the Dance Floor

It's 1977 and the disco is bumping — until someone turns up dead. Welcome to Last Dance: Putting the Gore in Groove, Sleuth Society Co.'s fully immersive murder mystery experience set in the golden age of platform shoes and bad decisions.

You'll be assigned a character with a backstory, a motive, and a few secrets. Over the course of the evening, you'll interrogate suspects, unravel an interconnected web of drama, and decide who you think pulled the trigger — or whatever they used.

Refreshments are included. The killer is among you. Everyone is a suspect.

This event is perfect for date nights, birthday celebrations, girls' nights out, or anyone who wants to do something in Ontario that's actually worth talking about.

Good Questions

Everything You're Wondering

Are refreshments really included?

Yes. Refreshments at Hamburger Mary's are included with every $55 ticket. Drinks are separate — and we highly recommend a cocktail or two for the vibes.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself and your detective instincts. Character materials will be provided at the door. Costumes are highly encouraged but never required.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely. Solo guests get fully integrated into the experience and make new friends fast. Half the suspects are strangers by design.

Is this good for a birthday party or group event?

It's one of the best group activities in Ontario — full stop. Reach out to us for group bookings of 8 or more.

Is it appropriate for all ages?

This event is recommended for guests 21+ due to the bar setting at Hamburger Mary's. It's theatrical, dramatic, and a little dark — in the best possible way.

What if I've never done a murder mystery before?

Perfect. This format is built for first-timers. You'll be guided the entire way. No experience necessary — just curiosity.
