The Night Someone Doesn't Make It Off the Dance Floor

It's 1977 and the disco is bumping — until someone turns up dead. Welcome to Last Dance: Putting the Gore in Groove, Sleuth Society Co.'s fully immersive murder mystery dinner experience set in the golden age of platform shoes and bad decisions.

You'll be assigned a character with a backstory, a motive, and a few secrets. Over the course of the evening, you'll interrogate suspects, unravel an interconnected web of drama, and decide who you think pulled the trigger — or whatever they used.

Dinner is included. The killer is among you. Everyone is a suspect.

This event is perfect for date nights, birthday celebrations, girls' nights out, or anyone who wants to do something in Rancho Cucamonga that's actually worth talking about.

Good Questions

Is dinner really included?

Yes. A full dinner at The Old Spaghetti Factory is included with every $65 ticket. Drinks are separate.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself and your detective instincts. Character materials will be provided at the door. Costumes are highly encouraged but never required.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely. Solo guests get fully integrated into the experience and make new friends fast. Half the suspects are strangers by design.

Is this good for a birthday party or group event?

It's one of the best group activities in Rancho Cucamonga — full stop. Reach out to us for group bookings of 8 or more.

Is it appropriate for all ages?

This event is recommended for guests 16+. It's theatrical, dramatic, and a little dark — in the best possible way.

What if I've never done a murder mystery before?

Perfect. This format is built for first-timers. You'll be guided the entire way. No experience necessary — just curiosity.

Seats Are Limited

This is an intimate experience — we cap attendance to keep the mystery tight. Once it sells out, it's gone.
