The Society

This isn't your grandmother's dinner theater.

Questions for the Detective

Murder Mystery Dinner FAQ

What is a murder mystery dinner?

A murder mystery dinner is an interactive evening where the guests are part of the story. A crime unfolds while dinner is served, and everyone questions suspects, gathers clues and votes on who did it. It’s not passive dinner theater: you are the detective, and you might be a suspect too.

Do I have to act?

Only if you want to. You can play an active character with a full backstory and motive, or join as an investigator and interrogate the room. Either way, you’re in the game.

How do tickets work?

Every public show is listed on our Events page with its date, venue and price. Tap Buy Tickets to check out online: some shows sell through our secure Stripe checkout and others through the venue’s Eventbrite page. Shows marked Coming Soon list who to contact for reservations.

Is dinner included?

It depends on the show and venue. Each listing on the Events page says what your ticket includes.

Where do you host murder mystery dinners?

Our operators host throughout Southern California, Northern California, San Francisco and New Orleans (see Events Near You). Upcoming cases include Temecula, Riverside, Redondo Beach and Sycamore Inn in Rancho Cucamonga, and past shows have taken place at Loft 84, Mimi’s Cafe, The Old Spaghetti Factory, Hamburger Mary’s and Sycamore Inn.

Can you host a private party or corporate event?

Yes. We take private events, corporate bookings and venue partnerships. Get in touch and we’ll plot something unforgettable for your group.

How do I hear about new events first?

Join the Case File list. We only email about Sleuth Society events, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
