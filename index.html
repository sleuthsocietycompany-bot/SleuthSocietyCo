The Society

This isn't your grandmother's dinner theater.

Forget passive performances and cheesy costumes. At Sleuth Society Co., you are the detective. You are the suspect. You are the story.

From the moment you arrive, you're transported into a fully immersive world — complete with character cards, secret motives, hidden clues, and a killer lurking somewhere in the room. Everyone has a motive. Only one is guilty. Can you solve the case before the night is over?

Choose your role: become an active character with a full backstory, or play investigator and interrogate the room. Either way, the game is always afoot.

The Method

How the Society Works

Every Sleuth Society experience is designed to pull you into the mystery from the first moment to the final reveal.

01
🎭

Choose Your Role

Pick an active character with a full backstory and motive, or play as an investigator. Either way, you're in the game.

02
📋

Receive Your Case File

Your character card, alibi, and secret intel arrive at your seat. Study them carefully. Someone at your table is lying.

03
🔍

Investigate

Question suspects, gather clues, form alliances — or plant false trails. Dinner is served while the mystery unfolds.

04
⚖️

Make Your Accusation

Cast your vote. Who had the motive? Who had the means? The detective with the correct accusation wins the night.
